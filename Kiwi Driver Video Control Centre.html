Publication verification rules

A video is Published only when every required supported account has an actual provider-level published result, a post ID or URL, and a publication timestamp. Scheduled, pending, draft, or unverified accounts keep the video incomplete. Unsupported account types are explicitly excluded with a reason, not silently counted as successful.

Collision prevention blocks new generation when the same brand has generating, generated, needs review, draft, scheduled, failed or blocked work awaiting resolution. A held brand always blocks generation. A retry must retain the original Airtable record and video/post IDs and be reconciled before any replacement is created.
